Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 13, 2026

NaviCav ("we", "our", or "the App") is a public-transport navigation application for Cavite Province, Philippines. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can manage it.

1. Information We Collect

Data Type Collected Purpose
Email address Yes Account creation, password reset, and support
Display name Yes Personalization of your profile
Profile photo Optional User-uploaded avatar displayed in the app
Device location Yes Navigation, route planning, and anonymous heatmap analytics
Route searches Yes Saving favorites, recent searches, and anonymous route analytics
Device information Bug reports only Attached to feedback to help us reproduce issues
Payment / financial data No —
Contacts, SMS, or call logs No —

2. How We Use Location Data

We access your device's location only while you are actively using the App (foreground). Location data is used for:

  • Navigation: Turn-by-turn directions and route planning between your origin and destination.
  • Anonymous Analytics: Aggregated, de-identified heatmaps to help city planners understand transit demand. No personally identifiable information is attached.

We do not collect location data in the background or when the App is closed.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Supabase (secure cloud infrastructure). We protect your data with:

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS / TLS) for all data in transit
  • Row-level security policies on all database tables
  • Secure authentication via Supabase Auth and Google OAuth 2.0

4. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — Authentication, database, and file storage (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Sign-In — Optional sign-in method (Privacy Policy)
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim / Photon — Map tiles and geocoding (Privacy Policy)

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile, saved routes, favorites, and recent searches are permanently deleted.
  • Your uploaded files (profile photos, bug report screenshots) are permanently deleted.
  • Community contributions (advisories, feedback) are anonymized — content is kept but your identity is removed.

6. Account Deletion

You may delete your account at any time through:

  • In the App: Profile → Personal Information → Danger Zone → Delete Account
  • By email: navicav.dev@gmail.com — send from your registered email address

Account deletion is processed immediately and all personal data is removed as described in Section 5.

7. Guest Mode

You may use NaviCav without creating an account ("Guest Mode"). In this mode, we do not collect any personal information. Location is used only for on-device navigation and is not stored on our servers.

8. Children's Privacy

NaviCav is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Your Rights

You may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (see Section 6)
  • Withdraw consent for location data collection via your device settings at any time
